🗂️ Best DAT Sources


          

            Here are the best places to get DAT files for your ROM Organizer Pro
            setup. These are the standard sources used by FinalBurn Neo, MAME,
            EmulationStation DE, LaunchBox, ClrMamePro, and RomVault.
          


          
No-Intro DATs (cartridge systems)

          

            Best for: NES, SNES, N64, GBA, DS, 3DS, Genesis
          

          

            Official:
            No-Intro DAT-o-MATIC
          

          

            Use these for clean retail dumps, naming standardization, and hash
            verification.
          


          
Redump DATs (disc systems)

          

            Best for: PS1, PS2, Saturn, Dreamcast, GameCube,
            Wii, Sega CD
          

          

            Official:
            Redump.org
          

          

            Use for CHD validation, BIN/CUE validation, and ISO verification.
          


          
FBNeo DAT

          
Best for: FinalBurn Neo arcade ROMs

          

            Official GitHub:
            FinalBurn Neo GitHub
          

          

            DAT folder:
            FBNeo DATs Folder
          

          

            Important: match DAT version to emulator version; arcade ROM sets
            are version-sensitive.
          


          
MAME DATs

          

            Best for: MAME, arcade validation, BIOS
            verification
          

          

            Official:
            MAME Downloads
          

          
Or generate directly with:

          
mame -listxml > mame.xml


          
RetroArch DAT collection

          

            Useful mega-pack:
            Libretro DATs
          

          

            Contains playlist DATs, No-Intro mappings, and arcade mappings.
            Great for ES-DE scraping and RetroArch playlists.
          


          
Recommended DAT structure

          
dats/
├── no-intro/
├── redump/
├── fbneo/
├── mame/
└── retroarch/

Best practice

          

            Use No-Intro for cartridge systems, Redump for discs, FBNeo for
            arcade ROMs, and MAME for BIOS validation. This gives better scraper
            matching, accurate naming, cleaner ES-DE libraries, and fewer
            duplicates.
          


          
Future upgrade
